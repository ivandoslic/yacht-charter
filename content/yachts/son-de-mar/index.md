# S﻿ON DE MAR

## O﻿verview

Experience the Adriatic in true elegance aboard the 45-meter Son De Mar, a striking motor sailer that blends timeless design with modern comfort. With space for up to 12 guests across six beautifully appointed cabins, this vessel is designed for those who appreciate refined living at sea.

The spacious interior offers a bright, stylish salon with panoramic views, while expansive decks invite outdoor living at its best—sun loungers, shaded dining, a foredeck jacuzzi, and a beach club area make every moment on board a pleasure.

Whether you’re savoring gourmet meals prepared by the onboard chef, relaxing in the sauna, or enjoying water toys like SeaBobs, paddleboards, and wakeboards, Son De Mar promises both excitement and indulgence. For those who like to stay active, there’s a compact gym, yoga mats, and even a massage table for complete wellness on the water.

With a professional crew of seven dedicated to personalized service, your journey through Croatia’s coast will be smooth, luxurious, and entirely unforgettable.
