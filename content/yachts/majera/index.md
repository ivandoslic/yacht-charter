# M﻿AJERA

## O﻿verview

Featuring a beach club, main deck master, jacuzzi and five airy cabins for eleven guests, 27m Majera delivers the ultimate in superyacht living in a small, stunningly designed package.

Delivered in 2024, this brand-new Sirena 88 is the perfect embodiment of clever small yacht design. Majera's high-volume, tri-deck design offers an abundance of space and light. Luxuriate in the rarity of a full-beam, main-deck master cabin with floor-to-ceiling windows and a private entrance to a foredeck with jacuzzi.

Revel in the huge 55 m2 flybridge entertainer's deck with bar and dining.

The clever features just keep coming, with a tender garage that transforms into a beach club, and convertible cabins to offer maximum versatility to charter groups. Even the yacht's performance is versatile, with top speeds of 25 knots balanced with an impressive fuel efficiency and stability at lower speeds.

Running with four experienced crew member and available for charter in Croatia, Majera offers the best of both worlds.
