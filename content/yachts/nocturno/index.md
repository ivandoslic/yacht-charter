# N﻿OCTURNO

## O﻿verview

Sailing Yacht Nocturno is a 48.2-meter luxury vessel that combines elegant design with modern amenities, offering an exceptional cruising experience along Croatia's Adriatic coastline. Accommodating up to 12 guests in six spacious cabins—including a master suite with a massage tub, a VIP cabin, two double cabins, and two convertible twin/double cabins—each features en-suite bathrooms, air conditioning, and state-of-the-art entertainment systems, ensuring a comfortable stay. 

The interior boasts a stylish main saloon with a dining area, bar, and lounge equipped with a TV, DVD player, and Wi-Fi, creating an inviting space for relaxation and socializing. Outdoor areas are equally impressive, featuring an aft deck with a cushioned platform and alfresco dining table, a bow deck with a sunbathing area, and a flybridge complete with a Jacuzzi, lounge area with bar, and ample sunbathing space. 

For those seeking adventure, Nocturno offers a wide array of water sports equipment, including a tender boat, Jet Ski, canoes, water skis, snorkeling gear, stand-up paddleboards, wakeboard, Seabobs, eFoil board, and fishing equipment. A dedicated crew of nine ensures personalized service, making every journey aboard Nocturno a memorable Adriatic adventure.
