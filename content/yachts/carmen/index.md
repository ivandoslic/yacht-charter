# C﻿ARMEN

## O﻿verview

With its striking militaristic exterior, Carmen commands attention wherever she sails—an iconic design that redefines luxury yachting. Beneath this bold facade lies an inviting, beach-house-inspired interior that exudes elegance and open-air living. Five spacious cabins accommodate up to 10 guests in absolute comfort, complemented by thoughtfully designed open-air spaces for seamless relaxation and entertainment.

Expansive decks are a haven for sports enthusiasts, offering activities like basketball and table tennis, while the foredeck Jacuzzi invites you to unwind in style. Carmen perfectly balances adventure and sophistication, promising a charter experience unlike any other.

Crafted by Aegean Yachts and scheduled for launch in January 2025, she will be ready to welcome her first guests in the Summer 2025 season.
