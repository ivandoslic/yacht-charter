# E﻿LIZABETH

## O﻿verview

A high-volume yacht for her size, the Princess Y72 offers a superb charter layout. She accommodates up to eight guests in four contemporary, light-filled staterooms, including a full-beam master that is reached via a private spiral staircase leading down from the salon. A VIP stateroom with ensuite spans the width of the yacht forward, while two twin cabins located midships can be converted into two doubles, and share an ensuite. All cabins have large windows that imbue this lower accommodation with an airy, luxurious atmosphere.

The sense of light and space continues up on the main deck, where huge panoramic windows wrap right around the salon, and the decor of floorboards, pale timber, and cream lounges consolidate the contemporary, airy design.

More entertaining space is found up on the flybridge, where a dining and sun pad area is completed with a barbeque, bar, and fridge, while the foredeck offers a magnificent lounge area curving around a small occasional table.

This brand-new Princess Y72 will be the only one of its kind available for charter in Croatia and Montenegro from summer 2022 onwards. She runs with an experienced crew of 3.
