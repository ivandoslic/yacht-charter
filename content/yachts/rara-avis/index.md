# R﻿ARA AVIS

## O﻿verview

Sailing Yacht Rara Avis is a 33-meter luxury vessel that combines traditional craftsmanship with modern elegance, offering an unparalleled cruising experience along Croatia's Adriatic coastline. Accommodating up to 12 guests in six spacious cabins—including a master suite, a VIP cabin, two double cabins, and two twin cabins—each features en-suite bathrooms, air conditioning, and ample storage space, ensuring a comfortable stay.  

The yacht's interior boasts a contemporary saloon with a large dining area, lounge bar, stylish sofas, and a state-of-the-art audio and video system, creating an inviting atmosphere for relaxation and socializing.  

Outdoor areas are equally impressive, featuring a spacious aft deck with an alfresco dining area, a flybridge equipped with a Jacuzzi, sun loungers, and a shaded lounge area, perfect for enjoying panoramic views of the Adriatic Sea.  

For adventure enthusiasts, Rara Avis offers a variety of water sports equipment, including a Jet Ski, SeaBob, water skis, wakeboard, paddleboards, kayaks, and snorkeling gear, ensuring ample opportunities for on-water fun.  

A dedicated crew of six is committed to providing exceptional service, tailoring each journey to the guests' preferences and ensuring a memorable and personalized sailing experience.  

Whether exploring secluded coves, indulging in gourmet dining on deck, or engaging in thrilling water activities, Sailing Yacht Rara Avis offers the perfect setting for an unforgettable Adriatic voyage.

**APA (Advance Provisioning Allowance):** 30% of charter fee paid with the balance for covering running costs during the charter

* Port and marina fee
* Fuel for jet ski and tender operated watersports
* National park entrances
* Custom fees and charter licenses if chartered outside of Croatia
* Food and drinks options, and anything else that guests might need while on charter
* A strict set of accounts is held by the captain throughout the charter and any funds not spent are refunded to the client in full.

**\*APA to be paid in cash on board or as a wire transfer with the last payment to a shipping agent’s account. If APA is to be paid as a wire transfer, commission of 1,5% for the shipping agent will be deducted from the APA.**
