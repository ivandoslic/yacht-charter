# B﻿ORA BORA

## O﻿verview

Motor Yacht Admiral 27, named Bora Bora, is a stunning 26.8-meter yacht available for luxury charters in the breathtaking waters of Croatia and Montenegro. Launched in 1992 and meticulously refitted in 2020, Bora Bora is in excellent condition. Guests are assured of exceptional service provided by a professional crew of four, including a Captain, a chef, a hostess, and a sailor. Bora Bora is the perfect choice for those seeking a memorable charter vacation.

Bora Bora's impressive interior configuration comfortably accommodates up to 8 guests in four luxurious cabins. The accommodations include a Master cabin, a VIP cabin, and two twin cabins, all with en-suite bathrooms. Guests can relax in spacious and elegant daytime seating areas both inside and outside the yacht. The main salon, with its exquisite design, is ideal for entertaining guests or enjoying intimate family gatherings. The dining area, adorned with classy décor, is sure to impress all guests. 

Motor yacht Bora Bora combines luxurious accommodations with a wide array of exciting water activities and fitness equipment, ensuring an unforgettable yachting experience in the stunning Adriatic region.
