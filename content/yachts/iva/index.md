# I﻿VA

## O﻿verview

Set sail on an exceptional yachting vacation aboard the Ferretti 960 IVA, a magnificent 29.20m (95'10") motor yacht berthed at Herceg Novi in Montenegro, offering luxurious charters in the Adriatic.

Accommodating up to 10 guests in five beautifully appointed ensuite cabins, her layout is ideal for families and couples. There's a spacious master cabin, two VIP cabins, and two twin cabins that conveniently convert to doubles for maximum flexibility.

Unwind and soak in the breathtaking scenery on IVA's expansive outdoor areas. Enjoy al fresco dining under the sun or host unforgettable gatherings on the spacious flybridge with its very own bar, or soak up on the beloved "Ferretti style" sea-level aft deck with lounge sun pads connected to the swim platform. Whether you seek relaxation or entertainment, IVA provides the perfect setting.

Designed with both aesthetics and functionality in mind, IVA effortlessly combines elegance with comfort cruising. Spanning just over 29 meters in length, with stabilizers both on anchor and underway, and reaching maximum speed of 30 knots, she is the ideal choice for leisurely island hopping throughout the Adriatic Sea. With MY IVA as your luxurious home away from home, every moment of your Adriatic escape will be extraordinary.
