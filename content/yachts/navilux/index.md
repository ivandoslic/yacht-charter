# N﻿AVILUX

## O﻿verview

Sailing yacht Navilux offers a premier yachting experience along the Adriatic coast, seamlessly blending elegance, performance, and comfort. As Croatia’s first premium-class motor sailer, this 37-meter yacht was custom-built to provide an extraordinary charter journey. Recently refitted, Navilux features a sleek steel hull, expansive teak decks, and an impressive sail plan that balances motor and sail cruising beautifully. Every detail aboard has been thoughtfully curated to provide a luxurious home at sea, perfect for those seeking a blend of adventure and relaxation.

Navilux accommodates up to twelve guests in six spacious, air-conditioned cabins—five with king-size beds and one with twin beds—all with en-suite bathrooms and refined interiors. Designed with contemporary flair and warmth, the interior includes custom artworks by Croatian artist Mirna Sišul, giving the yacht a unique cultural touch. The main salon is open and inviting, offering a sophisticated lounge, dining area, and a well-stocked bar, all equipped with entertainment systems to make onboard living as comfortable as possible. Whether guests want to unwind in privacy or gather for shared moments, Navilux provides the perfect setting.

Outside, the yacht’s multiple deck spaces invite guests to soak up the beauty of the Adriatic. The sundeck features a large jacuzzi and sun loungers, perfect for long afternoons under the Mediterranean sun. The aft deck is ideal for alfresco dining, while the forward area offers additional lounging space with uninterrupted sea views. A wide swimming platform at the stern provides easy access to the sea and serves as a launch point for an impressive collection of water toys.

For those who love to stay active or explore the water, Navilux is fully equipped with jet-skis, SeaBobs, paddleboards, wakeboards, kayaks, snorkeling gear, and more. Whether it’s a serene paddle at sunset or high-speed fun on the water, there’s something for every mood. A dedicated crew of up to eight ensures seamless service throughout your charter, including a skilled chef who crafts exquisite meals tailored to guest preferences, using only the freshest local ingredients.

Navilux is more than just a yacht—it’s a floating sanctuary where luxury meets the Adriatic lifestyle. Every journey aboard is tailored to your desires, whether you're sailing to remote bays, vibrant island towns, or simply enjoying the comfort of the yacht itself. With her stylish design, exceptional amenities, and experienced crew, Navilux promises an unforgettable charter experience along one of the world’s most breathtaking coastlines.
