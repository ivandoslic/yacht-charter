# C﻿ASABLANCA

## O﻿verview

MY Casablanca - traditional feeling intertwined with modern luxury

What does the word luxury mean? It means Casablanca! Luxury is the complete relaxation of your mind and body in a comfortable environment with all the opulence you can imagine. Casablanca provides incredible luxury with exceptional live aboard cabins and 250m2 of open area for leisure and sunbathing. MY Casablanca can accommodate 36 passengers in 18 magnificent ensuite cabins with service that will cater to your every need. Except for all the luxury you can imagine, Casablanca also provides for most relaxing moments in our sauna, wellness zone with massage and spacious lounge areas.

Our tailor-made cruise is just that - made to accommodate all your wishes and preferences, especially considering food, refreshments, and suggestions for our itinerary. We take pride in being at your disposal anytime during your cruise.

MY Casablanca offers you accommodation in 18 luxury cabins. Each cabin has two twin beds (90 x 200 cm) or one double bed (180 x 200 cm). All cabins are equipped with air-conditioning, LCD TV, free Wi-Fi, a worktable, mirror, safety deposit box, night lamps, wooden wallboards, chair, electricity supply, safety and fire alarm installations, mini bar, hairdryer and ensuite bathroom with shower cabin.
