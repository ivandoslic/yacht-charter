# O﻿LIMP

## O﻿verview

Luxury Yacht Olimp is a magnificent 49-meter vessel designed to deliver an unparalleled cruising experience along Croatia's stunning Adriatic coastline. Accommodating up to 20 guests in ten contemporary cabins, each features en-suite bathrooms, air conditioning, and modern amenities, ensuring a comfortable and luxurious stay. 

The yacht's interior boasts an elegant main saloon that combines a dining area with a stylish bar and lounge, equipped with a TV, DVD player, and Wi-Fi connectivity. The soothing minimalist design creates an inviting atmosphere for relaxation and socializing. 

Outdoor spaces are equally impressive. The aft deck features an open lounge area with sofas and coffee tables, perfect for enjoying morning coffee while cruising to the next destination. The expansive sun deck offers sun loungers, a Jacuzzi, and a bar, providing ample space for sunbathing and taking in the breathtaking views. A swimming platform with a staircase allows comfortable access to the sea for water activities. 

For adventure enthusiasts, Olimp offers a variety of water sports equipment, including a Jet Ski, stand-up paddleboards, water skis, a SeaBob, and inflatable rides such as a donut and banana. The exceptional crew of ten, led by owner and captain Anton, is renowned for their kindness and incredible hospitality, ensuring a personalized and memorable sailing experience.

Whether exploring secluded coves, indulging in gourmet dining on deck, or engaging in thrilling water activities, Luxury Yacht Olimp provides the perfect setting for an unforgettable Adriatic voyage.

**APA (Advance Provisioning Allowance):** 30% of charter fee paid with the balance for covering running costs during the charter

* Port and marina fee
* fuel for jet ski and tender operated watersports
* national park entrances
* custom fees and charter licenses if chartered outside of Croatia
* food and drinks options, and anything else that guests might need while on charter
* A strict set of accounts is held by the captain throughout the charter and any funds not spent are refunded to the client in full.

\***APA to be paid in cash on board or as a wire transfer with the last payment to a shipping agent’s account. If APA is to be paid as a wire transfer, commission of 1,5% for the shipping agent will be deducted from the APA.**

**CHARTER PRICE does not include:**

* Transfers from/to the airports, yachts, hotels, crew tip
